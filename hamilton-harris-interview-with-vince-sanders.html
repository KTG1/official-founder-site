The short version

Seven ideas that define the conversation.

  1. 01:2901

    Entrepreneurship began early

    Sanders connects his business instincts to growing up around entrepreneurs in Kansas City. Working from a young age taught him to observe people, take responsibility, and turn practical experience into judgment.

  2. 06:2602

    Entrepreneurs are problem-solvers

    For Sanders, entrepreneurship is less about having a title and more about seeing what can be improved. Progress comes from listening, learning quickly, and creating a useful answer for customers.

  3. 09:0603

    A personal mission became a company

    The story of CBD American Shaman begins with a family-inspired exploration of hemp. Sanders describes how research, experimentation, and a desire to share quality products became a business with a broader wellness mission.

  4. 30:2304

    Quality rests on clear principles

    The conversation highlights three ideas behind the brand: start with quality ingredients, use thoughtful production methods, and earn confidence through transparency. Testing and consistency turn those ideas into daily practice.

  5. 48:4805

    Education can move an industry

    Sanders reflects on industry advocacy and the value of sharing real experiences with decision-makers. His account presents education, collaboration, and persistence as ways entrepreneurs can help an emerging category mature.

  6. 1:14:1906

    Success is built, then shared

    His advice to aspiring entrepreneurs centers on action, curiosity, and resilience. Sanders defines success beyond a single financial result: it includes creating opportunity, helping people, and building something with lasting value.

  7. 1:17:2607

    The next generation has new tools

    Looking ahead, Sanders sees AI and robotics as expansive tools for young entrepreneurs. CBD American Shaman’s own use of AI illustrates his larger point: lasting companies stay curious and keep adapting.
